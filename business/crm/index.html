WhatsApp CRM

What a WhatsApp CRM is

WhatsApp itself stores conversations, not customers. A CRM adds what a business needs around the conversation: who this person is, what they bought, which colleague owns the relationship, what was promised and when to follow up. A WhatsApp CRM does that with WhatsApp as a channel, usually alongside email, Instagram, web chat and calls.

Two kinds of product

WhatsApp-first inbox platforms

Built around messaging: a team inbox, broadcast tools, a chatbot builder and a light contact database. Fast to set up and strong on WhatsApp features such as templates, buttons and catalogs. The customer record is simpler than in a full CRM, and most offer connections to the big CRMs.

General CRMs with a WhatsApp channel

A full sales or service CRM where WhatsApp is one more inbox. The right choice when the CRM is already the centre of the business and WhatsApp should simply appear on the contact's timeline. Check the plan: the WhatsApp integration often sits in a paid tier.

Checklist before you pay

  • Is it on the official Business Platform? Look for "Meta Business Partner", "Solution Partner" or the Cloud API. Tools that ask you to scan a QR code to "connect WhatsApp Web" are not, and put the number at risk.
  • How is it priced? Per seat, per contact, per conversation, or a mix. Model your own numbers.
  • Is there a per-message markup on top of Meta's fees?
  • Can you keep using the Business app on the same number (coexistence), if you need that?
  • Which integrations are native: your shop system, calendar, helpdesk, existing CRM.
  • Can you export contacts and conversation history if you leave?

Questions people ask

Does WhatsApp have its own CRM?

No. The WhatsApp Business app has labels and a business profile, which is as close as it gets. A WhatsApp CRM is third-party software connected to your number through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Can I connect WhatsApp Web to my CRM?

Not in a way WhatsApp permits. Browser extensions that read WhatsApp Web and copy chats into a CRM work against WhatsApp's terms and see everything in your tab. The supported route is connecting the number to the Business Platform, where the CRM receives messages officially.

Will my team still be able to use the phone app?

Normally a number on the Business Platform is no longer used in the Business app. Meta's coexistence option, offered by some partners, lets one number work in both, with limits: broadcast lists are switched off and the Windows app cannot be a linked device. Check that your vendor supports it before you move.

What does a WhatsApp CRM cost?

Expect a per-user or per-seat subscription, sometimes with a cap on contacts or conversations, plus Meta's per-message fees. The cards below list the entry price of each option as the vendor states it.

Can I see who on my team answered which customer?

Yes. That is the core of a shared inbox: named agent logins, chat assignment, internal notes and reports per agent, none of which exist in WhatsApp Web.
