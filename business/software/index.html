How to choose

  1. Name the job. A shared inbox for a team, a chatbot, campaigns, WhatsApp inside an existing CRM, or a bare API for your developers. Few tools are best at more than two.

  2. Count what the price counts. Seats, contacts, conversations or messages. A plan that looks cheap per seat can be expensive per contact, and the other way round.

  3. Look for the markup. Meta's per-message fees are the same for everyone. Some vendors pass them on at cost, others add a percentage or a fixed amount per message. It matters more from 1 October 2026, when Meta starts charging for replies beyond 1,000 a month; a vendor page that still says "service conversations are free" is describing the past.

  4. Use the trial with real chats. Connect a spare number, send yourself templates, and have two colleagues work the inbox for a week.

Not there yet? The free WhatsApp Business app on WhatsApp Web carries a small business a long way.

Questions people ask

What is the best WhatsApp Business software for a small business?

The one that fits how you work. A shop that mainly answers questions needs a shared inbox with a free trial and no per-message markup. A business that wants bots and campaigns needs a flow builder and broadcast tools. Start from the "best for" column, then compare the starting price and message fees.

Do I pay WhatsApp as well as the software?

Usually yes. Meta charges per delivered message on the Business Platform: templates always, and from 1 October 2026 also replies within the 24-hour window beyond 1,000 free ones a month per number. The software subscription is separate, and some vendors add their own markup per message.

Can I try these platforms for free?

Most have a free trial or a free plan; the table shows what each vendor states. You normally need a phone number that is not active in the regular WhatsApp app to test with.

Is it safe to connect my WhatsApp number to third-party software?

Yes when the software uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform, which all platforms in this comparison do. Avoid tools that work by scanning a WhatsApp Web QR code; they are unofficial and risk a ban.

Does this site earn money from these links?

From some of them, if an affiliate programme exists and we joined it; those links are marked on the page. Prices and limits are copied from each vendor's own page whether or not they pay us.
