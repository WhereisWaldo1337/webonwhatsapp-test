Frequently asked questions

Is webonwhatsapp.com the official WhatsApp Web?

No. This is an independent guide. The official WhatsApp Web is at web.whatsapp.com and nowhere else. We cannot sign you in, and we never ask for a QR scan, a phone number or a verification code.

Is WhatsApp Web free?

Yes. WhatsApp Web and the WhatsApp desktop apps are free and use the same account as your phone. You only need an internet connection on the computer.

Does my phone need to be online to use WhatsApp Web?

Only while you link the computer. After that WhatsApp Web works on its own, even when your phone is off. If your phone does not use WhatsApp for more than 14 days, linked devices are logged out.

Can I use WhatsApp Web without a phone at all?

No. A WhatsApp account is always registered on a phone, and a computer can only be added as a linked device from that phone.

How many computers can I link?

You can link up to four devices to one account, in addition to your phone. They can be browsers, the Windows or Mac app, tablets or a second phone.
