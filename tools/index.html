Free WhatsApp tools

Small tools for people who use WhatsApp for work. They run in your browser: no account, no install, and nothing you type is sent to us.

The tools

  • WhatsApp link generatorMake a wa.me click-to-chat link with a pre-filled message. Nothing is stored.
  • Auto-reply menu generatorA full "reply 1 for..., 2 for..." menu for your business, with every reply written.
  • WhatsApp API cost calculatorMeta's message fees per month for your volumes, including the October 2026 rule.
  • Chat button generatorA floating click-to-chat button for your site: one HTML snippet, no script.
  • Text formatterBold, italic, lists and quotes with a live preview, ready to paste.

What these tools do not do

None of them connects to your WhatsApp account, asks you to scan a QR code or sends messages for you. Tools that do that by driving WhatsApp Web are against WhatsApp's terms and get accounts banned, and this site does not offer or list them. Sending messages automatically is what the official WhatsApp Business Platform is for; the software comparison lists the platforms built on it.
