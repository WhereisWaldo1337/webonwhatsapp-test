Rules that trip people up

  • No space inside the markers. *bold* works, * bold * does not.
  • Quotes and lists start the line. The >, - or 1. must be the first thing on the line and be followed by a space.
  • Monospace uses three backticks on each side and can span several lines; one backtick on each side marks a short piece of inline code.
  • A marker in the middle of a word is left alone, so file_names_like_this stay as they are.

For business messages

Formatting earns its place in customer messages: a bold order number, a short list of next steps, a quoted line of the customer's own question. Keep it sparse; a message with every second word in bold reads like spam. The same characters work in quick replies in the WhatsApp Business app, and bold, italic, strikethrough and monospace also work in the body of message templates on the Business Platform.

Questions people ask

How do I write bold text in WhatsApp?

Put an asterisk on both sides of the text, like *this*. WhatsApp shows it in bold once the message is sent.

Can I combine bold and italic?

Yes. Nest the characters, for example *_important_*, and WhatsApp shows the text bold and italic.

Why does my formatting not work?

The characters must touch the text: "* text *" with spaces inside does nothing. Each style also has to close on the same line, and the list and quote characters only work at the very start of a line, followed by a space.

Does formatting work on WhatsApp Web?

Yes. The same characters work on the phone, on WhatsApp Web and in the desktop apps, because the formatting is part of the message itself.

Is the text I type here stored?

No. The preview is made inside your browser and nothing you type is sent to this site.
