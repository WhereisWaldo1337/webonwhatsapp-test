        What makes a menu work

        • Four or five options, no more. The list has to fit on a phone screen without scrolling. Everything else goes through "talk to a person".
        • Always offer a person. WhatsApp expects businesses to give customers a way to reach a human, and customers who feel trapped in a menu stop answering.
        • Put the most-asked thing first. Look at your last fifty chats; the order of the menu should follow what people actually ask.
        • Say how fast you reply, and keep the promise. A menu that ends in silence is worse than no menu.

        To bring customers into the menu, put a chat button on your website or a click-to-chat link in your bio.

        The free route: by hand in the WhatsApp Business app

        You can type a menu like this into the free WhatsApp Business app yourself.         What the free app does not do: it cannot read "2" and answer by itself. The greeting and the away message are automatic, every reply to a menu choice is one tap by you, and the phone with the Business app has to be online. Answers that go out on their own need a bot: your own for $29.99, or a monthly platform from the chatbot guide.
